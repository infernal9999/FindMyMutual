    <h2>About the Platform</h2>
    <p>Welcome to <strong>Find My Mutual</strong>, a free and user-friendly platform designed specifically for railway employees seeking mutual transfers. With the help of <strong>Google Sheets</strong> as our secure database and <strong>Google Apps Script</strong> managing backend operations, we provide a seamless experience. The platform is hosted on <strong>GitHub Pages</strong> and uses an <code>iframe</code> to integrate the deployed Google Apps Script link, ensuring a flexible and shorter URL.</p>
