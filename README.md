# **Find My Mutual**

Welcome to **Find My Mutual**, a **free platform** dedicated to simplifying mutual transfers for Railway employees. Our solution combines the power of **Google Sheets** and **Google Apps Script** to ensure a seamless experience. The site is hosted on **GitHub Pages**, with the backend deployed via Google Apps Script embedded in an `iframe` for a shorter and more reliable address.

---

## **Discover the Benefits**

### 1. Hassle-Free Account Creation  
Set up your account effortlessly in just a few steps.

### 2. Quick Password Recovery  
Regain access to your account easily with our recovery feature.

### 3. Email Notifications  
Stay updated about potential matches by selecting the *"Get notified by email?"* checkbox.

### 4. Flexible Station Code Inputs  
Enter multiple station codes of your preferred division for broader match possibilities.

### 5. Easy Details Editing  
Update your details effortlessly after logging in.

### 6. Direct Connections  
Access contact details of potential matches directly.

### 7. Seamless Account Deactivation  
Take control with a simple account deactivation option.

---

## **How It Works**

1. **Create an account** through our streamlined registration process.  
2. **Log in** and input your preferred station codes.  
3. **Browse matches** or wait for email notifications about suitable connections.  
4. Reach out directly to your mutual match for further discussion.

## **Technical Details**

- **Google Sheets**: Serves as the backend database.  
- **Google Apps Script**: Manages operations and connects the database with the frontend.  
- **GitHub Pages**: Hosts the main webpage and integrates the deployed link using an `iframe`.
