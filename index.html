About Find My Mutual

Welcome to Find My Mutual, a platform designed to make mutual transfers easier for railway employees. Register yourself, set your preferences, and explore potential matches for transfer opportunities. Our app ensures a smooth and hassle-free experience.

How It Works

1. Register your details on the platform.
2. Set your preferences for transfer locations.
3. Browse through potential matches and connect with them.
