About Find My Mutual

Find My Mutual helps Indian Railway employees across all 18 Zones and 73+ Divisions discover compatible partners for Mutual Exchange Transfers (Paraspar Sthanantaran).

Our v2.0 engine automatically normalizes 7th CPC Pay Levels (GP ₹1800–₹4800), standardizes Railway Departments, checks IREM Para 310 roster compatibility, and discovers both Direct 2-Way Swaps and Triangular 3-Way Cycles.

How It Works

  1. Register Your Profile: Enter your 6-letter HRMS ID, 7th CPC Pay Level / Grade Pay, Department, Current Station/Division, and multiple Preferred Stations/Divisions.
  2. Explore Tiered Matches: Instantly view Tier 1 (Exact Station Swaps), Tier 2/3 (Division Swaps), and Tier 4 (Triangular 3-Way Cycles).
  3. Daily 5 AM Email Alerts: Enable automatic daily email notifications whenever a new matching railway employee joins.
  4. Connect & Apply on HRMS: Reach out via 1-click WhatsApp or Email and submit your joint application under IRHRMS-ESS → Mutual Transfer Request.

Key Railway Board Mutual Transfer Rules

  • Same Cadre & Grade Pay: Both employees must belong to the same Department, Cadre, and Pay Level (Master Circular No. 24).
  • Seniority Rule (IREM Para 310): Both employees take the seniority of the junior partner (whichever is lower). Basic pay remains protected under FR 22(I)(a)(1).
  • Roster Category (RBE 08/2019): UR/OBC matches with UR/OBC, SC with SC, and ST with ST in promotional posts; relaxed against Direct Recruitment Quota (DRQ) vacancies on bottom seniority.
  • No Backtracking (RBE 200/2009): Once submitted and approved in HRMS, mutual transfer requests cannot be withdrawn.
