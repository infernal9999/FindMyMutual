About Find My Mutual

Welcome to Find My Mutual, a platform designed to make mutual transfers easier for Indian Railway Employees. Register yourself, set your preferences, and explore potential matches for transfer opportunities. Our app ensures a smooth and hassle-free experience.

How It Works

1. Kindly create an account using our simple and streamlined registration process.
2. Log in to explore potential matches or make any necessary edits to your details.
3. Alternatively, you may wait to receive email notifications about suitable connections.
4. Once a match is found, feel free to reach out directly for further discussions.
