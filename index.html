About Find My Mutual (Unofficial Portal)

Find My Mutual is an independent, unofficial community tool built to help Indian Railway employees search and connect with compatible mutual transfer partners across all 18 Railway Zones and 73+ Divisions.

Important Disclaimer: This website is not affiliated with Indian Railways or the Ministry of Railways. It only helps you discover a willing mutual partner. All official mutual transfer applications, consents, and approvals must be completed on the official Indian Railways HRMS Portal (hrms.indianrail.gov.in).

How It Works in 3 Steps

  1. Create Profile: Enter your 6-letter HRMS ID, Pay Level, Department, current posting, and optional profile photo.
  2. Find Compatible Partners: Compare 2-Way Station/Division matches and 3-Way Triangular cycles side by side.
  3. Apply on Official HRMS: Contact your matched partner via WhatsApp/Email and submit your official request on HRMS.
